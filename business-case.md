# Business case: ten-slide content plan

This is reviewable slide content, not a rendered deck. All baselines come from the supplied synthetic leads; see `reports/lead-analysis.md`. Proposed results are targets.

## 1. Customer problem
Small businesses face slow quotes, paperwork and installation follow-ups. Of 5,000 supplied leads, 368 activated (7.36%); mean activation took 17.62 days and 3.01 follow-up calls.

## 2. Initial customer
Pilot professional offices: 1,247 leads, 11.07% activation, Rs 6,575 median monthly activated bundle. Clinics are one example, not a separately proven segment. Micro retail is a later price-sensitive experiment: 2,709 leads, 4.06% activation, Rs 2,470 median bundle and 22.73% recorded churn.

## 3. Customer value
One phone-friendly journey: describe needs, understand a package, edit a transparent quote, consent to KYC and track a saved order. Guided discovery remains usable if the model is unavailable.

## 4. Illustrative offer
Five-person office: Biz Fibre 300 (Rs 1,499) plus five email accounts (5 x Rs 125) totals Rs 2,124/month before taxes, with zero listed setup fees and a 12-month minimum. Prototype prices use catalogue minimums; availability needs confirmation. Tighter budgets can trade speed for coverage of more needs.

## 5. Recommendation approach
AI proposes profile details and explains eligible services. Users confirm changes. Server rules own product eligibility, dependencies, quantities and pricing. No model fine-tuning is needed for the initial structured catalogue.

## 6. Verification approach
Prior consent, local English OCR, mock registry status and field/date comparisons. Clear per-document results; unreadable, expired or mismatched documents require review. Optional external AI needs separate consent. Extracted text does not prove authenticity.

## 7. Partners and assistance
Retain field/channel partners for document discrepancies, site feasibility and installation coordination. The current app saves review cases locally; no team is notified. A later authenticated queue can support assignment and partner attribution.

## 8. Rollout
Synthetic internal testing -> small professional-office pilot with assistance -> review failures/unit economics -> micro-retail and tier-2 experiments -> wider rollout. Real identity/provisioning connections follow tested adapters and production authentication/security work.

## 9. Metrics and economics
Baseline: Rs 15,053.37 blended CAC and 15.22% recorded six-month churn. Pilot targets: >=12% activation conversion, <=10 days mean activation, <=Rs 12,000 CAC, <=10 minutes median digital checkout and 20% relative churn reduction against a comparable observed cohort. Include model, hosting, manual-review, partner and installation costs before claiming savings. Digital checkout and physical activation are separate metrics.

## 10. Risks and next decision
Risks include bad extraction, language misunderstandings, unsuitable packages, unsafe document handling and provider outages. Controls include deterministic pricing, confirmed changes, prior consent, local processing and explicit review states. Request a controlled pilot; live AI evaluation, complete localization, real provisioning and production controls remain before launch.
