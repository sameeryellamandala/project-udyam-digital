# Customer acquisition evidence

Generated from the supplied synthetic leads dataset. These are historical baselines, not measured results from this prototype. Regenerate with `npm run analyze`. No business names, contacts or government identifiers are included.

## Baseline

- 5,000 leads; 368 activations (7.36%).
- Mean lead-to-activation: 17.62 days; mean follow-up calls: 3.01.
- Blended acquisition cost: Rs 15,053.37 per activation (all lead acquisition spending / activations).
- Median monthly bundle among activated customers: Rs 6,900.
- Recorded six-month churn: 15.22% of 368 activated customers with a recorded Y/N outcome. The supplied synthetic outcomes are used as given; this does not establish real cohort maturity.
- NPS: -25.27 (promoters 9-10 minus detractors 0-6, divided by activated respondents).

## Registration association

Activation is 9.56% for leads marked as having GST and 2.05% for those without it. This is an association in synthetic data, not evidence that obtaining GST causes conversion. Support customers without GST through unrestricted bundles and assisted review.

## Product implications

Prioritize clear quotes, quick document feedback and transparent installation status. Use the segment comparison to select a pilot, then measure conversion, activation time, acquisition cost and churn against the baseline. Do not present proposed improvements as achieved savings.
