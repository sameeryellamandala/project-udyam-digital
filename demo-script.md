# Three-minute demonstration script

Use fictional details and the supplied image pack. Confirm `npm ci`, `npm start` and `npm test` beforehand. Never show `.env` or private browser storage in a recording.

## 0:00-0:20 — The problem
Explain that small businesses face slow quotes, paperwork and repeated installation follow-ups. Show the synthetic-data notice. State whether live AI is configured or the demo uses guided mode.

## 0:20-1:00 — Discovery and advice
Enter a clinic or retailer name. Ask for internet and business email for a small team with a monthly budget. Apply advisor suggestions, review the fields, then show the recommended package. Explain the product reasons and catalogue-based pricing.

## 1:00-1:25 — A transparent quote
Change a quantity or add-on. Show monthly subtotal, setup fee, contract and typical install days. Mention taxes are excluded and availability still needs confirmation. Show a dependency/budget warning if demonstrating an invalid combination.

## 1:25-2:20 — Smart KYC
Add a fictional contact/address matching the selected sample. Explain consent before entering identifiers. Choose local reading; upload the matching sample documents. Run checks. Show per-document outcomes and any registry/date mismatch. A supplied document may read cleanly while its registry is cancelled; explain why that correctly requires assisted review. Do not claim that extracted text proves authenticity.

## 2:20-2:45 — Order and tracking
Save the order for review or scheduling. Refresh the page to show that the order number and status persist. Explain that real installation and messaging are not connected and no appointment has been booked.

## 2:45-3:00 — Business evidence and privacy
Reference `reports/lead-analysis.md` for the measured synthetic baseline. State the proposed pilot targets separately. Show privacy choices and briefly mention local OCR, optional external processing and seven-day retention.
